The challenge

Asymmetric value, asymmetric risk.

Connectd connects startups and scaleups with the investors, board advisors and NEDs they need to grow. The core tension was asymmetric value: founders needed credible capital and expertise urgently, investors needed curated dealflow without noise, and advisors needed the right board seats, not volume. Getting the matching wrong in either direction destroyed trust on both sides, and on a network handling £50M+ in funding connections, trust is near impossible to rebuild. On top of that, 58% of founders were abandoning onboarding at step 3, role and stage selection, before the platform had a chance to show them any value.

System map. Founders, investors and advisors: three distinct goals and mental models, mapped onto one coherent platform before a single screen was designed.

My approach

Map the system before touching the UI.

I mapped the full three-sided system, user states, data flows, matching triggers and trust signals, before designing a single screen, then ran discovery interviews with VC partners, angel investors, founders and NEDs to surface how each group actually thought about their goals. From there I defined a role-based information architecture, one platform, three radically different surfaces, built on a single component system with 80% reuse across all user types. The matching interface surfaces weighted compatibility signals in plain language, giving both sides confidence without exposing the algorithm or creating false precision. A/B tests on messaging, card density and CTA placement, run with the data team, showed the explanation line, not the percentage, was what actually drove connection requests.

What I built

Nine gates to one progressive screen.

Across the platform I designed role-based dashboards for founders, investors and advisors on one shared component system; a matching interface that moved compatibility signals above the fold after scroll maps showed users never reached them, lifting cross-side engagement 65% in six weeks; a founder onboarding redesign that replaced nine mandatory upfront questions with three plain-language ones, cutting step-3 drop-off from 58% to 19% and 30-day churn by 34%; plus a trust and credibility layer (verification badges, profile completeness, the handshake moment), a fundraising tracker and a board recruitment flow. The product is live at connectd.co.
